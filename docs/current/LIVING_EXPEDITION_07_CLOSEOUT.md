shortcuts do not measure full-route pacing, long-term attachment, or replay value.

assess whether choosing among the current three companions and remembering their
shared history is meaningful enough to justify a bounded Growth, Release, And
Habitat Legacy proof. A functional GO alone does not justify forced retirement,
legacy rewards, or a broader roster; address a concrete experience gap first if
that evidence is absent. Release/legacy, Vein Whiskers, and a fourth species stay
directional. #52/#53 remain explicitly deferred slice-03 polish.
