# Reference Standard

## Primary Direction

### Visual Direction B - Modular Cave Terrain

File: `references/visual/visual_direction_b_modular_cave.png`

Use for:

- side-view underwater cave composition
- modular rock floors, walls, ceilings, ledges, and arches
- clear cyan-blue water
- readable open swim paths
- sparse salvage props and coral decoration
- large AI-friendly terrain chunks

Do not use for:

- final map layout
- exact player, sub, crate, or fish designs
- copied composition
- baked-in collision decisions

## Supporting Direction

Dave-the-Diver-like references may be used for player scale, open-water feel, and side-view exploration mood. Do not copy its exact art style, map geometry, UI, characters, or branded elements.

Existing OceanGame assets may be used as concept references:

- `player_diver_swim_sheet_v1.png`: diver concept and silhouette only; not final animation standard.
- `research_sub.png`: strong sub concept; likely needs scale/style normalization.
- `research_boat.png`: strong boat/base concept; likely needs scale/style normalization.

## Anti-Reference

Avoid the old retro/SNES cave look:

- no heavy pixel-art cave walls
- no dark low-readability palette for the first prototype
- no dense noisy terrain detail
- no retro HUD or arcade identity unless deliberately chosen later
