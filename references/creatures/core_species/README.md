# Core Species Silhouette Review

Date: 2026-08-06

Status: Directional concept references. These images are not approved runtime
sprites and do not authorize a third-species implementation before the Living
Expedition 03 owner gate.

## Shared Direction

- Orthographic side-view silhouettes use one diver scale reference.
- Dark navy massing tests recognition before color and texture.
- Cyan marks describe anatomy, motion, aim, or sensed information; they are not
  an elemental type language.
- Each sheet tests a neutral pose, locomotion or warning posture, deliberate
  field action, and adaptation-level anatomy change.
- Future sprite work should simplify these shapes substantially at gameplay
  scale rather than tracing the generated detail.

## Species Review

### Spark Ray

Strongest read: a broad living mount whose low rider position and bracing limbs
make its two control modes understandable.

Refinement risk: the tall dorsal shape leans too close to a shark. A second pass
should flatten that profile, keep the kite-wing mass dominant, and integrate the
rider saddle into living anatomy rather than implying equipment.

### Veil Cuttle

Strongest read: the eye-led mantle and paired ribbon fins immediately separate
Mica from Kite and make observation feel bodily rather than tool-like.

Refinement risk: the longest veil poses exceed practical cave clearance. Runtime
silhouettes should curl or compress the ribbons during close follow and reserve
full extension for deliberate Reveal Trace and Drift Lens actions.

### Silt Hound

Strongest read: the head-down listening posture, walking rays, and sudden folded
swim profile establish a new expedition rhythm. This remains the recommended
third-species direction because it connects creature choice to prospecting,
recipes, authored daily candidates, and map revisits.

Refinement risk: stiff legs can read as a terrestrial insect. The next pass
should make all six supports visibly flexible fin rays, reduce hard joints, and
keep the whisker fan distinct from ordinary barbels at thumbnail scale.

### Breaker Mantis

Strongest read: asymmetrical arms, elevated eyes, warning display, windup, and
recoil communicate a deliberate real-time combat partner before any UI appears.

Refinement risk: the current hammer is oversized and rock-like. A second pass
should use a compact biomineralized shell club with a clearer spring-loaded
forearm, preserving force without turning the species into a comedy weapon.

## Recommendation

Keep all four body plans as the production core, with targeted silhouette
revision before color work. Review Silt Hound first after the current two-species
player gate. Defer Breaker Mantis implementation until enemy health, warning,
recovery, nonlethal handling, and wildlife-resource rules are stable enough to
support its role.

The four later directional species in the design bible should not receive image
or runtime batches until these four remain recognizable together at actual
gameplay zoom.
